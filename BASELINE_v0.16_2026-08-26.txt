1. PURPOSE

This baseline records the approved global governed-analytics exclusion of five exact Tempo work-item keys:

- TEMPO-54
- TEMPO-92
- TEMPO-106
- TEMPO-111
- TEMPO-112

The exclusion is implemented in the production data build and reinforced at browser data ingestion so the governed analytical population is consistent before calculation, enrichment, aggregation, filtering, searching, rendering, reconciliation, or export.

2. BUSINESS MEANING

TEMPO-54 is Absence (Out Of Office). TEMPO-106 is Daily total working time (Legal Tracking).

TEMPO-92, TEMPO-111, and TEMPO-112 collectively represent HR daily-registration and vacation records. The available source data does not establish which individual key corresponds to which of those purposes, so this baseline does not infer a per-key mapping.

These records are not governed delivery or operational effort and contribute zero to governed analytics.

3. MATCHING AND PIPELINE RULE

Candidate Jira or Tempo keys are trimmed, converted to uppercase, and then compared by exact equality with the complete five-key exclusion set. Prefix, substring, numeric, or fuzzy matching is prohibited. Valid near matches such as TEMPO-540 and valid operational work such as TEMPO-30 remain included.

The exclusion is applied immediately after source ingestion and before:

- classification as Engineering, Operations, planned, or unplanned
- Jira or hierarchy enrichment
- calculation or aggregation
- reconciliation
- facet and filter population
- table, chart, KPI, tooltip, or summary rendering
- search results
- CSV export

The same governed population is used throughout the application. Later filters cannot reintroduce excluded rows.

4. AFFECTED ANALYTICS

The exclusion applies globally to all governed current and future analytical outputs, including:

- Logged Hours and Billable Hours
- Actual Hours, Annual Capacity Remaining, and Annual Capacity Utilization numerators
- Engineering, Operations, planned, unplanned, Account, Team, Assignee, Program, Skill, Priority, Status, Target Release, and Release breakdowns
- Accounts, People, Backlog Worked On, and Delivery Progress details
- work-item counts, distributions, trends, summaries, tooltips, search results, and CSV exports
- reconciliation outputs
- Jira-derived current backlog activity and any future demand candidate population

5. VERIFIED DATA IMPACT

Source window: 02-Jan-2026 through 05-Aug-2026.

All five excluded keys in the raw worklog source account for 3,301 rows, 3,794.149 logged hours, and 419.959 billable hours. TEMPO-54 and TEMPO-106 were already excluded by the previous implementation. The incremental effect of adding TEMPO-92, TEMPO-111, and TEMPO-112 to the managed-team governed population is:

- worklog rows: 23,725 to 23,404 (-321)
- logged hours: 37,949.500 to 37,571.793 (-377.707)
- billable hours: 33,946.580 to 33,573.563 (-373.017)
- distinct work items: 2,517 to 2,514 (-3)
- Engineering logged hours: unchanged at 31,275.948
- Operations logged hours: 6,673.552 to 6,295.845 (-377.707)

After the complete five-key exclusion, Engineering plus Operations reconciles exactly to 37,571.793 managed-team governed logged hours.

The five excluded keys have no rows in the current Jira backlog source and therefore remove 0.000 Original Estimate hours and 0.000 Remaining Estimate hours in the current snapshot. The rule remains authoritative for any future Jira-derived demand population.

6. PROTECTED BOUNDARIES

Capacity is person/calendar based and is not issue-key based. The exclusion does not alter capacity sources or denominators:

- annual workforce capacity: 65,470.000 hours
- weekly planned capacity: 52,665.000 hours

The independent Bug Triage queue remains governed solely by its dedicated Jira export. It is not filtered by the Tempo analytical exclusion and remains at 46 distinct Jira keys in the current snapshot.

Version 2.1 forecasting implementation remains parked. Forecasting governance now records the five-key rule only as an input-boundary requirement; no forecast resolver, allocation behavior, Release Cycle dashboard behavior, or other forecasting implementation was introduced.

7. IMPLEMENTATION

The exclusion set, normalization, reasons, masks, and partitioning are centralized in `scripts/governed_exclusions.py`. Both the production builder and the legacy preparation path consume that authority. `web/app.js` derives its browser-side guard from generated metadata rather than maintaining a second hard-coded exclusion set.

Generated metadata records the exact exclusion set and per-key source impact for auditability. Production output was rebuilt in `web/data.js`.
