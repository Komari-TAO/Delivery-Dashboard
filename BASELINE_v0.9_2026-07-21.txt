7. ACCOUNT DISTRIBUTION

Account Distribution represents engineering Tempo effort attributable to valid Jira Accounts.

- The treemap contains only valid Jira Accounts.
- `(No Account)` is not permitted as an Account Distribution tile.
- Operational TEMPO-* activities are excluded from the treemap.
- Operational TEMPO-* activities remain included in governed operational and total logged effort.
- Non-TEMPO engineering work whose Jira Account cannot be resolved is excluded from the treemap and reported separately.
- The footer derives from the exact same unresolved-engineering subset excluded from the treemap.
- Footer values remain dynamic: the Jira work-item count uses distinct normalized Jira keys and logged hours retain the approved displayed formatting.
- The footer is hidden when the exception population is zero.

Approved footer wording:

"Account attribution note: {distinct engineering Jira issue count} engineering Jira work items ({logged hours} h) could not be attributed from the current Jira source extract. These items remain included in governed logged effort. Operational TEMPO-* activities are excluded."

8. ACCOUNT ATTRIBUTION GOVERNANCE

- Tempo remains authoritative for logged effort.
- Jira remains authoritative for Account metadata.
- Account is mandatory for normal Jira work items.
- A Jira work item present in the platform extract with blank Account is a data-quality or technical exception. It must not be masked by an invented Account value or parent inheritance.
- A Jira key absent from the current extract is a source-coverage limitation, not proof that the Jira ticket does not exist, and must be distinguished from a present-but-blank Account.
- The current implementation uses a direct Jira Account first. Immediate-parent Account fallback is used only when the directly logged Jira key is absent from the current Jira extract, a parent is present, and the parent Account is nonblank.
- Recursive ancestor traversal, configurable hierarchy traversal, and metadata-driven hierarchy traversal are not implemented.

- Capacity uses Master Date exclusively.
- Tempo worklogs remain independent from Jira Status.

- Terminal Jira work does not remove historical Tempo effort.
