PROJECT STATE SUMMARY
- Dashboard architecture remains a static web app driven by generated `web/data.js` and rendered by `web/app.js`.
- Core governed semantics from v0.3 remain active:
  - Organizational/workforce Team is governed by Assignees CSV.
  - Capacity Team is governed by Weekly Capacity plus Assignees CSV.
  - Delivery Progress Team represents Jira ticket ownership.
  - Backlog Worked On Team represents Tempo contributor activity in the selected scope.
  - Week logic is governed by Master Date, not independent ISO logic.
  - TEMPO-106 and TEMPO-54 remain globally excluded from delivery analytics.
  - Default Status behavior excludes Jira Done-category statuses unless the user explicitly changes the Status filter.

  - Jira metadata enriches Tempo worklogs but does not determine whether logged hours exist.
  - Team attribution for governed delivery scope still comes from Assignees CSV.

BUSINESS RULES CODIFIED
- Logged Hours governance:
  - Logged Hours is Tempo-sourced, not Jira-sourced.
  - Valid Tempo worklogs inside the selected Date Range are included after governed exclusions.
  - Jira issue key is only the relationship used to enrich Tempo rows with Jira metadata.
  - Missing or unmatched Jira metadata must not remove logged effort.
  - Operational TEMPO-* work remains included as Logged Operations, except for globally excluded operational keys.
  - TEMPO-106 and TEMPO-54 remain globally excluded.
  - Managed Delivery scope is determined through Assignees CSV team mapping.
  - Rows outside managed Delivery scope because the assignee/team cannot be governed through Assignees CSV must be reported, not silently hidden.
- Assignee normalization governance:
  - Dot-format Tempo/Jira assignee names can resolve to canonical Assignees CSV names when they refer to the same governed person.
  - `ignacio.mangas` resolves to `Ignacio Mangas`.
  - `andres.rojas` resolves to `Andrés Rojas`.
  - The normalized canonical person inherits Team/Skill/Role only from Assignees CSV.
  - Marina Petrova and Yuliya Kandukova are not normalized or mapped and remain unmanaged/excluded unless added to Assignees CSV.
- Program Distribution is a reconciliation view, not only a pie chart.
  - Program-mapped Logged Hours must include only work resolved to governed Program labels.
  - Governed Logged Total is the Tempo logged-hours total in the governed scope after global operational exclusions.
  - Unmapped / Non-Program Logged Hours is the difference between Governed Logged Total and Program-mapped Logged Hours.
  - Reconciliation must be transparent and traceable.
- Program reporting must not hide unmapped work.
  - Previously unmapped or non-program work remains visible through the Unmapped / Non-Program total and validation log.
- Program values must be normalized to governed labels.
  - Raw Jira Program codes or variants are not the display contract.
- Planned vs Unplanned reporting is label-driven.
  - Jira labels are the authoritative source for Planned/Unplanned classification.
  - Parent and ancestor label inheritance is allowed for classification where applicable.
  - Jira label quality directly limits reporting completeness.
- Account Treemap is an effort-distribution view.
  - Area represents Logged Hours.
  - Percentages are filtered-scope percentages.
  - Missing Account is reportable and must not be suppressed.
- Filter controls are UI affordances over the existing governed filter semantics.
  - Compact picker UX must not alter Team, Status, Program, Account, or date governance.
- Delivery Health sort order is governed by risk severity:
  - Overdue / Critical
  - Due soon / At Risk
  - Healthy
  - No due date / Not applicable / Blocked
  - Completed
