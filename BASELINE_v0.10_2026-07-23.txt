9. IMPLEMENTATION BOUNDARIES

Product Module intentionally:

- is not filterable;
- is not calculated;
- is not aggregated;
- is not inherited from parent issues;
- is not used by KPIs;
- is not used by charts;
- is not used by reconciliation;
- is not used for grouping;
- is display-only metadata.

The MVP also continues not to implement recursive hierarchy traversal, configurable hierarchy traversal, metadata-driven hierarchy traversal, support activities, Support Account classification, inferred Jira metadata, or invented Account values.
