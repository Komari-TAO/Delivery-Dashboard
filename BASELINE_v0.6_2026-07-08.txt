2. IMPLEMENTED GOVERNANCE

Governed Logged Hours
- Governed Logged Hours = all valid Tempo worklogs within the selected Date Range after governed exclusions and after managed-scope enforcement.
- Current managed governed Logged Hours in the generated snapshot: 32,658.766 hours.

Tempo as source of truth
- Tempo is authoritative for:
  - Logged Hours
  - Billable Hours
  - Worklog Date
  - Last Worklog Date
  - operational TEMPO activity
- Jira metadata may enrich a Tempo worklog, but Jira metadata must not decide whether logged effort exists.

Jira as metadata enrichment
- Jira enriches Tempo worklogs with:
  - Program
  - Account
  - Status
  - Priority
  - Target Release
  - Fix Version
  - Delivery metadata
  - Due Date
  - Start Date
  - hierarchy
  - Issue Type
  - Remaining Estimate
  - Original Estimate
  - technical-debt indicator
- Missing Jira metadata must classify work as blank, unmapped, or unclassified rather than silently removing effort.

Assignees CSV governance
- Assignees CSV remains authoritative for:
  - Team
  - Skill
  - Role
  - Availability
  - canonical assignee naming
- Team attribution must come from Assignees CSV unless a visual explicitly uses Jira ownership semantics or Tempo activity context.

Weekly Capacity governance
- Weekly Capacity remains the authoritative source for weekly planned capacity.
- Capacity still maps selected Date Range to Weekly Capacity through governed internal week metadata.
- Capacity formulas were not redefined during this implementation phase.

Master Date governance
- Master Date remains the source of truth for:
  - internal Week Number
  - workday boundaries
  - reporting calendar alignment
- Internal week values remain present in generated worklogs and capacity logic.

Global exclusions
- TEMPO-106 remains globally excluded.
- TEMPO-54 remains globally excluded.
- These exclusions apply across analytics, KPIs, tables, reconciliation, utilization, backlog activity, and Delivery Progress.

Assignee normalization
- Canonical assignee lookup remains active.
- Dot-format and accent-insensitive normalization resolves equivalent names to the governed Assignees CSV identity.
- Validated examples:
  - `ignacio.mangas` -> `Ignacio Mangas`

Managed vs unmanaged scope
- Managed scope means Tempo worklogs whose assignee/team can be governed through Assignees CSV and the valid governed Team dimension.

Release Cycle governance

Long Date Range semantic warning
- A semantic warning appears on the Remaining Demand vs Logged Effort by Team chart when the selected Date Range exceeds 31 days.
- The warning explains that:

  - Remaining Demand reflects current open Jira work
  - shorter operational windows are preferable for workload pressure analysis
- This warning is a governance safeguard, not a calculation change.

4. KPI TERMINOLOGY

- `Annual Workforce Capacity`
  - Total governed annual workforce capacity from Assignees CSV.
- `Actual Hours`
  - Governed Logged Hours consumed in the selected Date Range.
- `Annual Capacity Remaining`
  - Annual workforce capacity minus governed Logged Hours in the selected Date Range.
- `Annual Capacity Utilization`
  - Governed Logged Hours in the selected Date Range divided by annual workforce capacity.

Intended terminology rule
- Logged Hours is the canonical governed effort term.
- Capacity KPIs remain annual-capacity semantics, not weekly forecast semantics.

7. KNOWN LIMITATIONS

- Annual Capacity KPI semantics
  - Annual workforce capacity is an annual organizational attribute, not a period-specific forecast or weekly remaining-capacity metric.

- Weekly Capacity granularity
  - Capacity still operates at Weekly Capacity granularity internally.
  - Partial Date Ranges therefore map to weekly capacity windows rather than a fully redesigned day-level capacity model.

- Remaining Demand interpretation over long periods
  - Remaining Demand is a current Jira open-work measure.
  - Logged Effort is cumulative over the selected Date Range.
  - Over long periods, the chart is useful for context but should not be read as a strict forecast-pressure metric.

- Future forecasting postponed to Version 2
  - Remaining Estimate forecasting and future-looking planning views are not part of the frozen MVP.

- Browser/CDP validation was not re-run during this baseline creation task
  - This file is documentation-only and does not perform new browser artifact generation.

- Some Jira metadata remains unmatched
  - Missing Account, Program, labels, Status, or other Jira fields still produce unmapped or unclassified work, which is visible by governance rather than hidden.

- Some source text still contains encoding artifacts from upstream CSV content
  - This does not change governed calculations but remains a presentation limitation.

8. KNOWN ACCEPTED DECISIONS

- Annual Workforce Capacity KPI intentionally represents annual workforce capacity from the Assignees CSV.

- Team Utilization and the People table use Weekly Capacity as the operational selected-period capacity model.

- Remaining Demand vs Logged Effort intentionally compares:
  - current Jira Remaining Estimate
  - governed Tempo Logged Effort during the selected Date Range
  - This is an operational workload view, not an estimation-accuracy view.

- Long Date Range warning is intentionally explanatory only.
  - It does not modify calculations.

- Week Number has been removed from the UI but remains internally for:
  - Master Date governance
  - Weekly Capacity mapping
  - weekly trend grouping
  - validation

- Engineering Work Mix intentionally excludes Operations from the engineering denominator while preserving reconciliation.

- Unmanaged assignees remain intentionally excluded from Governed Logged Hours until they become part of the governed Assignees dataset.
