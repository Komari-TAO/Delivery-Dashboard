Business hint: Compares planned and unplanned delivery work using distinct work items, supporting visibility into planning discipline.

Program Distribution

Tempo logged hours by Program

Business hint: Shows where governed Tempo hours landed by business program, including mapped and unmapped effort.

Bug Severity Distribution

Current operational Bug Triage queue by severity

Business hint: Summarizes the current operational Bug Triage queue by severity; this is an inventory view, not a Tempo-effort view.

Account Distribution

Tempo logged hours by account

Business hint: Shows how delivery effort is distributed across customer accounts; tile size represents logged hours and account tiles can be selected to filter.

Team Utilization

Capacity, logged hours, variance, and utilization rate

Business hint: Compares planned weekly capacity with logged hours by team to show variance and utilization.

Remaining Demand vs Logged Effort by Team

Outstanding Jira demand by delivery scope; Tempo logged effort by selected period.

Business hint: Compares outstanding Jira remaining estimate with logged effort by team to highlight demand carried versus effort spent.

Backlog Priority

Created backlog by priority in selected range

Business hint: Shows the priority mix of backlog items created in the selected range, making incoming demand visible.

Engineering Work Mix

Governed logged effort by engineering work category.

Business hint: Splits governed engineering hours into Bugs, Technical Debt, and Rest; operational Tempo work is excluded.

Work Item Types

Tempo worklog count by Jira issue type

Business hint: Shows which Jira issue types account for the most Tempo worklog rows, indicating activity volume by type.
